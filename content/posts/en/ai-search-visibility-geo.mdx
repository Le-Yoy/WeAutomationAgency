> **The short answer:** More of your customers now get their answers from ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google's AI Overviews than from a list of blue links. Getting your business *cited inside those AI answers* is called **GEO** (Generative Engine Optimization) or **AEO** (Answer Engine Optimization). It's not a nice-to-have anymore — Google's "People Also Ask" is now almost entirely AI-generated, and about half of B2B buyers start their research inside an AI chatbot. Here's how it works, and how to actually get found.

A prospect told me last month he'd "researched three agencies" before our call — and he'd never opened Google. He asked ChatGPT. That's the shift, in one sentence: **if the AI doesn't mention you, you don't exist to that buyer.** Here's what's happening and what to do about it.

## What is AI search — and what are GEO and AEO?

When someone types a question into ChatGPT, Perplexity, or sees Google's AI Overview at the top of the results, an AI writes the answer and **cites a handful of sources.** Being one of those cited sources is the whole game.

- **GEO (Generative Engine Optimization):** optimizing so generative AI engines *quote and cite* your content.
- **AEO (Answer Engine Optimization):** the same idea, framed around "answer engines."

Same goal, different acronym: **be the answer, not just a link.**

## Why this is urgent in 2026 (not next year)

The ground has already moved:

- Google's **"People Also Ask" boxes are now ~97–100% AI-generated**, up from around 12% a year earlier. The fastest-growing part of the results page is now AI-written.
- Roughly **half of B2B buyers now begin vendor research inside an AI chatbot** more often than Google — and a large share call AI search their *primary* source.

<Callout>
Classic tricks — chasing featured snippets, stuffing keywords, buying links — do less and less. If your business isn't structured to be *quoted by an AI*, you're invisible in the part of search that's growing fastest.
</Callout>

## SEO vs GEO vs AEO: what's the difference?

You don't replace SEO — you extend it. Here's how they compare:

| | **SEO** | **GEO / AEO** |
|---|---------|----------------|
| Goal | Rank a page in the blue links | Get cited inside an AI answer |
| Who reads it | A human scanning results | An AI summarizing an answer |
| Wins with | Keywords, backlinks, authority | Clear answers, sourceable facts, structure, brand mentions |
| Measured by | Rankings, clicks | Citations/mentions in AI answers |
| Still matters? | Yes | Yes — increasingly |

## How do you get your business cited by AI answers?

This is the practical part. AI engines favour content they can lift a clean, attributable answer from:

1. **Answer the question directly, up top.** Lead each page with a 2–4 sentence answer an AI can quote verbatim.
2. **Use real, sourceable facts** — numbers, dates, comparisons, definitions. AI cites specifics, not fluff. (Never invent them.)
3. **Structure clearly** — question-style headings, short paragraphs, tables, lists. Machines parse structure.
4. **Add schema/structured data** so engines understand what your page is.
5. **Build brand mentions and authority** — the more your business is talked about across the web, the more AI trusts and cites it.
6. **Make sure your content is server-rendered** — most AI crawlers don't run JavaScript, so anything loaded by script is invisible to them.

GEO isn't just writing; it's **engineering**: structured data, AI-answer monitoring, citation tracking, and automation that keeps it current.
