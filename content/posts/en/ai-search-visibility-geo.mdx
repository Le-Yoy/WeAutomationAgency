> **The short answer:** More of your customers now get their answers from ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google's AI Overviews than from a list of blue links. Getting your business *cited inside those AI answers* is called **GEO** (Generative Engine Optimization) or **AEO** (Answer Engine Optimization). It's not a nice-to-have anymore — Google's "People Also Ask" is now almost entirely AI-generated, and about half of B2B buyers start their research inside an AI chatbot. Here's how it works, and how to actually get found.

A prospect told me last month he'd "researched three agencies" before our call — and he'd never opened Google. He asked ChatGPT. That's the shift, in one sentence: **if the AI doesn't mention you, you don't exist to that buyer.** Here's what's happening and what to do about it.

## What is AI search — and what are GEO and AEO?

When someone types a question into ChatGPT, Perplexity, or sees Google's AI Overview at the top of the results, an AI writes the answer and **cites a handful of sources.** Being one of those cited sources is the whole game.

- **GEO (Generative Engine Optimization):** optimizing so generative AI engines *quote and cite* your content.
- **AEO (Answer Engine Optimization):** the same idea, framed around "answer engines."

Same goal, different acronym: **be the answer, not just a link.** If you have seen "AI GEO" in a search, that is the same thing: making your business visible and quotable in AI-generated answers.

If the acronyms themselves are new to you, [this short explainer of SEO, GEO, AEO and LLMO](/blog/seo-vs-geo-vs-aeo-ai-visibility) sorts them out in plain English. Then come back: the rest of this post is the practical part.

## Why this is urgent in 2026 (not next year)

The ground has already moved:

- Google's **"People Also Ask" boxes are now ~97–100% AI-generated**, up from around 12% a year earlier (AlsoAsked data). The fastest-growing part of the results page is now AI-written.
- Roughly **half of B2B software buyers now begin vendor research inside an AI chatbot** more often than Google — up from ~29% a year earlier (G2's 2026 *Answer Economy* report), with a large share calling AI search their *primary* source.

Classic tricks — chasing featured snippets, stuffing keywords, buying links — do less and less. If your business isn't structured to be *quoted by an AI*, you're invisible in the part of search that's growing fastest.

## SEO vs GEO vs AEO: what's the difference?

You don't replace SEO — you extend it. Here's how they compare:

| | **SEO** | **GEO / AEO** |
|---|---------|----------------|
| Goal | Rank a page in the blue links | Get cited inside an AI answer |
| Who reads it | A human scanning results | An AI summarizing an answer |
| Wins with | Keywords, backlinks, authority | Clear answers, sourceable facts, structure, brand mentions |
| Measured by | Rankings, clicks | Citations/mentions in AI answers |
| Still matters? | Yes | Yes — increasingly |

## How do you get your business cited by AI answers?

This is the practical part. AI engines favour content they can lift a clean, attributable answer from:

1. **Answer the question directly, up top.** Lead each page with a 2–4 sentence answer an AI can quote verbatim.
2. **Use real, sourceable facts** — numbers, dates, comparisons, definitions. AI cites specifics, not fluff. (Never invent them, and [fact-check anything AI wrote](/blog/google-fact-check-ai-content-before-publishing) before it goes live.)
3. **Structure clearly** — question-style headings, short paragraphs, tables, lists. Machines parse structure.
4. **Add schema/structured data** so engines understand what your page is.
5. **Build brand mentions and authority** — the more your business is talked about across the web, the more AI trusts and cites it.
6. **Make sure your content is server-rendered** — most AI crawlers don't run JavaScript, so anything loaded by script is invisible to them.

### October 2026 update: what ChatGPT reportedly looks for

In October 2026, part of the instructions behind ChatGPT's new model (GPT-6) leaked publicly, including the section on how it searches the web. It isn't officially confirmed, but it matches what we see in practice:

- **Trusted, relevant and recent sources first.** For anything time-sensitive, it wants a page with a **clear, visible date**.
- **Every claim gets a citation**, ordered by how well the source supports it.
- **Reddit is used for opinions, reviews and recommendations.** If people never talk about you there, you're missing from a big part of the "who should I pick?" answers.
- **Local businesses:** for "near me" questions it combines a business directory lookup with a web search, and it's told not to invent businesses. If you're not listed properly somewhere, you simply don't exist for it.

What to do with that: put a visible "last updated" date on your key pages, keep your Google Business Profile and directory listings complete and consistent, and get mentioned on trusted sites and in real community discussions, not just on your own site.

### October 2026: Google gives AI answers more links

On October 15, 2026, Google said links inside AI Mode and AI Overviews will [appear higher up and more often](https://9to5google.com/2026/10/15/google-ai-mode-overviews-links/), with richer "Dive Deeper" cards at the end of answers. It is also testing a section at the top for the one or two official sites that complete a task or hold specific information. For a business, being the clear official source on your own topic matters more now.

## How do you measure your visibility in ChatGPT?

Since October 14, 2026 we log every AI bot hit on our own site (ChatGPT-User, GPTBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot and others). The "-User" bots are the interesting ones: they mean an AI fetched our page live to answer a real person's question. Clicks are harder to see. According to [Tomek Rudzki's analysis at Peec AI](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/chatgpt-traffic-drop-appears-to-be-a-tracking-problem/593157/), ChatGPT started showing many more direct links in answers in early October, 54% of them pointing to homepages, often without a referrer, so visits get filed as "direct" traffic. That is why we added a "ChatGPT / AI" option to the "Where did you find us?" question in our lead form: it is the only real click attribution we have.

Since October 24, 2026 we also ask ChatGPT our target questions every week through an API and log which sources it cites. On the first run, it cited us for none of our 8 target keywords: that's our baseline. I explain the setup in [our Claude SEO workflow](/blog/claude-seo-workflow).
