> **The short answer:** In 2026, a basic no-code chatbot runs **$0–500 to set up and $29–99/month.** An agency-built one with real integrations is **$1,000–5,000 to build and $50–250/month.** A fully custom AI system tied into your CRM, booking, and internal tools runs **$5,000–20,000+.** A WhatsApp chatbot adds roughly **$890+ setup and $99+/month** on top of Meta's own message fees. Below: what actually drives the price, and how to know which one you need.

Half the sales calls I get open with the same question: *"Houssam, just tell me what this costs."* Most articles dodge it — "it depends," then a demo booking. I hate that. So here are the real numbers, where they come from, and the traps to watch for.

## How much does an AI chatbot cost in 2026?

Here's the honest range, by type:

| Type | Setup | Monthly | Best for |
|------|-------|---------|----------|
| **No-code / DIY** | $0–500 | $29–99 | Testing, simple FAQ bots |
| **Agency-built no-code** | $1,000–5,000 | $50–250 | SMBs who want it done right |
| **Custom AI + integrations** | $5,000–20,000+ | $200–2,000 | CRM, booking, internal systems |
| **WhatsApp add-on** | ~$890+ | ~$99+ *(plus Meta fees)* | WhatsApp-first businesses |

If you're a small business handling fewer than ~500 conversations a month, budget **$50–100/month all-in** for a platform bot. If you need it wired into how your business actually runs, you're in build territory.

## What actually drives the price?

Five things, in order of impact:

1. **How many conversations you handle.** More volume = more AI usage cost.
2. **Which AI model runs it.** Smarter models cost more per message.
3. **How many channels and integrations.** Website only is cheap. Website + WhatsApp + CRM + calendar is not.
4. **Buy vs build.** A platform subscription vs a custom system are different worlds.
5. **Who sets it up.** DIY is cheapest upfront — and most expensive if it's built wrong and you redo it.

## No-code vs custom — which do you actually need?

**No-code** (ManyChat, Chatfuel, Make, and friends) is perfect if you want to answer FAQs, capture leads, and route people. You can be live in days for very little.

**Custom** makes sense the moment the bot needs to *do* things inside your business — check real availability, book an appointment, pull an order status, update your CRM. That's where a chatbot stops being a toy and starts making you money. It costs more because it's real engineering, not a template.

My rule of thumb: **start no-code to prove it works, go custom once it's clearly paying for itself.**

## What about WhatsApp chatbots?

WhatsApp is where a lot of businesses actually talk to customers, so it deserves its own note. On top of the bot itself, you pay:

- **Meta's fees** — but you get **1,000 free service conversations per month**, which covers a lot of small businesses.
- **A BSP (provider) fee** — roughly $30–150/month depending on volume.
- **The AI model cost** if it's an AI bot — often $10–50/month for an SMB.

Account verification with Meta takes about 1–5 business days, and a proper setup with workflows and testing usually takes 1–2 weeks. Anyone promising a serious WhatsApp AI bot "in an hour" is selling you a toy.

## The hidden costs nobody mentions

- **Setup, template, and onboarding fees** some platforms tack on separately.
- **Meta's per-conversation charges** on WhatsApp, on top of your provider's markup.
- **The redo tax** — the biggest one. A cheap bot built wrong, that annoys customers or can't connect to your tools, costs you far more than doing it right once.

## So is it worth it?

If a bot saves your team hours a week, answers customers at 2am, and turns "just browsing" into booked calls — a $100–250/month system pays for itself fast. The question isn't really *"how much does it cost?"* It's *"how much is it costing you to answer the same questions by hand, and to miss the messages you never see?"*
