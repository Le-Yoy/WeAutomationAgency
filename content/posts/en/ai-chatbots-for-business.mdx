## What is an AI chatbot, really?

An AI chatbot is a program that holds a real conversation with your customers — on your website, WhatsApp, Instagram, or Messenger. Unlike the clunky "press 1 for sales" bots of the past, a modern AI chatbot understands plain language, remembers context, and can actually *do* things: check availability, book a meeting, pull an order status, or hand off to a human when it matters.

Think of it as a tireless team member who answers instantly, at 2am, in any language, and never forgets to follow up.

## What can an AI chatbot actually do for your business?

The ones that pay for themselves do at least one of these:

- **Answer repetitive questions** — hours, pricing, "do you do X?" — so your team stops retyping the same replies.
- **Qualify and capture leads** — ask the right 3–5 questions, then route hot leads to you.
- **Book appointments** — connect to your calendar and fill your diary while you sleep.
- **Recover lost sales** — follow up with people who almost bought.
- **Support customers 24/7** — in multiple languages, instantly.

The mistake is treating a chatbot as a toy that answers FAQs. The value shows up when it's tied into how your business actually runs.

## The main types of chatbot (and which you need)

| Type | What it is | Best for |
|------|-----------|----------|
| **Rule-based** | Fixed "if this, then that" flows | Simple FAQs, menus |
| **AI (LLM-powered)** | Understands free language, reasons | Real conversations, support, sales |
| **Custom AI + integrations** | AI tied into your CRM, calendar, systems | Booking, orders, real operations |
| **WhatsApp chatbot** | Any of the above, on WhatsApp | Businesses whose customers live on WhatsApp |

Most businesses start with an AI bot on their site and add [WhatsApp](/blog/whatsapp-chatbot-cost) once it proves itself.

## Is an AI chatbot worth it?

If it saves your team hours a week, answers customers instantly, and turns "just browsing" into booked calls, a $100–250/month system pays for itself fast. 2. **Start no-code** to prove it works.
