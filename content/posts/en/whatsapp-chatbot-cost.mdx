> **The short answer:** A WhatsApp chatbot in 2026 costs roughly **$890+ to set up and $99+/month** for the bot itself, plus a **BSP (provider) fee of ~$30–150/month**, **AI model costs of ~$10–50/month** for a small business, and **Meta's per-message fees on the template messages you send first**. **Replying to customers who message you is free** inside the 24-hour window. Setup usually takes **1–2 weeks**. Below: the full breakdown and the traps.

WhatsApp is where a huge number of businesses actually talk to customers — so a WhatsApp chatbot is often the highest-ROI bot you can build. But the pricing confuses everyone, because there are *layers*. Here's every layer, in plain English. ## What you actually pay for

A WhatsApp chatbot isn't one bill — it's four:

| Layer | Typical cost | What it is |
|-------|--------------|-----------|
| **The bot / setup** | ~$890+ setup, ~$99+/mo | Building and running the chatbot itself |
| **BSP (provider) fee** | ~$30–150/mo | Access to the official WhatsApp Business API |
| **AI model** | ~$10–50/mo (SMB) | The "brain" if it's an AI bot |
| **Meta fees** | Per template message* | Only on messages *you* start (marketing, utility, authentication). Replies to customers are free inside 24 h |

\*Since July 1, 2025 Meta bills **per delivered template message** (no more "conversations"), based on the message type and your customer's country. Meta only changes prices on Jan 1, Apr 1, Jul 1 and Oct 1, so check the [official WhatsApp rate card](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing) for your country before you commit. (Checked October 13, 2026.)

## What's free on WhatsApp, and what isn't?

This is where most people get it wrong. Meta doesn't charge for the conversation anymore, it charges for **template messages**: pre-approved messages a business sends first.

**Free (per Meta's official rules, October 2026):**
- **Every reply to a customer who messaged you**, as long as you answer within **24 hours** of their last message (the "customer service window"). For most small businesses, that's the bulk of what a chatbot does.
- **Utility templates** (order updates, appointment reminders) sent while that 24-hour window is open.
- **Everything for 72 hours** when the customer reached you through a Click-to-WhatsApp ad or your Facebook Page button, if you reply within 24 hours.

**Paid:**
- **Marketing templates** (promotions, offers, newsletters): the most expensive type and with no volume discount. Depending on the country, roughly 1 to 16 US cents per message, with Western Europe at the top of the range.
- **Utility and authentication templates** sent outside the window (reminders, one-time codes): much cheaper, and they get cheaper with volume.

So a bot that mainly **answers** customers can have a Meta bill close to zero. A bot that **pushes** campaigns to thousands of people is where the Meta bill grows.

## How long does setup take?

- **Meta Business verification:** ~1–5 business days.
- **Technical connection via a BSP:** ~1–3 days.
- **Building the flows + testing:** ~1–2 weeks depending on complexity.

Anyone promising a *serious* WhatsApp AI bot "in an hour" is selling you a toy. A real one — connected to your calendar, CRM, or catalogue — takes proper setup. That's the difference between a bot that annoys customers and one that books them.

## No-code vs custom WhatsApp bot

- **No-code** (ManyChat and similar) gets you live fast for a low monthly fee — great for FAQs, lead capture, and simple flows.
- **Custom** is worth it when the bot must check real availability, take a booking, or update your systems. See our full [tool comparison](/blog/best-ai-chatbot-tools) and [how to build one](/blog/how-to-build-ai-chatbot).

## The hidden costs to watch

- **Template and onboarding fees** some providers add on top.
- **Marketing templates:** sending promotions to thousands of contacts is where Meta's fees add up, plus your provider's markup on each message.
- **The redo tax** — a cheap bot that can't connect to your tools costs more than doing it right once.

## Is a WhatsApp chatbot worth it?

If your customers already message you on WhatsApp, yes — almost always. Instant replies at any hour turn "are you open?" into booked business, and since replies to customers are free, it can start paying for itself from month one.
