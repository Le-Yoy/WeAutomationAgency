> **The short answer:** A WhatsApp chatbot in 2026 costs roughly **$890+ to set up and $99+/month** for the bot itself — plus a **BSP (provider) fee of ~$30–150/month**, **AI model costs of ~$10–50/month** for a small business, and **Meta's per-conversation fees** (you get **1,000 free service conversations every month**). Setup usually takes **1–2 weeks**. Below: the full breakdown and the traps.

WhatsApp is where a huge number of businesses actually talk to customers — so a WhatsApp chatbot is often the highest-ROI bot you can build. But the pricing confuses everyone, because there are *layers*. Here's every layer, in plain English. (For chatbot pricing in general, see [how much an AI chatbot costs](/blog/ai-chatbot-cost); for the big picture, the [complete AI chatbot guide](/blog/ai-chatbots-for-business).)

## What you actually pay for

A WhatsApp chatbot isn't one bill — it's four:

| Layer | Typical cost | What it is |
|-------|--------------|-----------|
| **The bot / setup** | ~$890+ setup, ~$99+/mo | Building and running the chatbot itself |
| **BSP (provider) fee** | ~$30–150/mo | Access to the official WhatsApp Business API |
| **AI model** | ~$10–50/mo (SMB) | The "brain" if it's an AI bot |
| **Meta fees** | Per conversation* | Meta's own charge — *1,000 free service conversations/month* |

\*Meta's conversation pricing has been shifting through late 2026 — always check current rates for your country before you commit.

## What are Meta's free conversations?

This trips people up. Meta gives every business **1,000 free service conversations per month** — conversations started by a customer messaging you. For a small business, that often covers most or all of your volume, so your real Meta bill can be near zero at the start. Marketing-initiated conversations (you messaging them first) are charged separately.

## How long does setup take?

- **Meta Business verification:** ~1–5 business days.
- **Technical connection via a BSP:** ~1–3 days.
- **Building the flows + testing:** ~1–2 weeks depending on complexity.

Anyone promising a *serious* WhatsApp AI bot "in an hour" is selling you a toy. A real one — connected to your calendar, CRM, or catalogue — takes proper setup. That's the difference between a bot that annoys customers and one that books them.

## No-code vs custom WhatsApp bot

- **No-code** (ManyChat and similar) gets you live fast for a low monthly fee — great for FAQs, lead capture, and simple flows.
- **Custom** is worth it when the bot must check real availability, take a booking, or update your systems. See our full [tool comparison](/blog/best-ai-chatbot-tools) and [how to build one](/blog/how-to-build-ai-chatbot).

## The hidden costs to watch

- **Template and onboarding fees** some providers add on top.
- **Meta's per-conversation charges** beyond your free 1,000, plus your provider's markup.
- **The redo tax** — a cheap bot that can't connect to your tools costs more than doing it right once.

## Is a WhatsApp chatbot worth it?

If your customers already message you on WhatsApp, yes — almost always. Instant replies at any hour turn "are you open?" into booked business, and the free-conversation allowance means it can start paying for itself from month one.
